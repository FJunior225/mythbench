Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Summary

MythBench's publishing tool is used only by the MythBench creator to post MythBench's own videos to its own accounts. It does not collect data from the public or from other TikTok users.

What we access

When the MythBench creator connects their TikTok account, the tool receives, through TikTok's official APIs:

  • Basic profile information for the connected account (display name and avatar), to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to that same account, used only when the creator chooses to publish a video.

The same applies to the creator's own YouTube and Instagram accounts when connected.

How access is stored

Access tokens are stored by our integration provider, Composio, to keep the connection active. They are used only to publish MythBench's own videos and read basic account information.

What we don't do

  • We do not access, collect or store data about other TikTok users, followers or viewers.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use cookies or analytics on this website.

Removing access

The connected account can be disconnected at any time from TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, then Manage app permissions) or by deleting the connection in Composio. Once disconnected, the stored tokens are deleted.

Children

This tool is not directed at children and is not used by anyone other than the MythBench creator.
