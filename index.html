MythBench

GTA 6 myths, put to the test.

MythBench is a gameplay channel that answers one GTA 6 question at a time by testing it in the game: the fastest car, the best heist payout, whether you can survive that jump. New tests go up daily as short videos, with a full roundup on YouTube every Sunday.

Where to watch

YouTube: @MythBench

About this site

MythBench uses a small personal publishing tool to upload its own videos to its own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. The tool is used only by the MythBench creator and is not offered to the public.
